## Agreed first source pack selection

Root directed regional/stat variation through the existing regional pack machinery. Encounters owns `RPG_ACCEPTED_SOURCE_PACK_ASSIGNMENTS` and measured formation checks. The first seven placements use six accepted source bodies: Forest at Rootfall south stump hollow; Beetle at Gorge Watch northeast roots; Shale at outer tarn track and the southern ridge; Lava at Clinker southern approach west; Spider at Marchgate south bramble; Wasp at Mossbound west bramble. Marchgate's specified bramble pack belongs to Vellenwood and satisfies the current Spider region restriction. Three existing Fire packs retain their content stats and share the accepted Lava asset.

This selection adds actual whole-body variety at existing settings. It does not create extra body silhouettes through names or palette changes. Pack centres, radii, existing dressing and member save IDs remain frozen; root owns world activation after formation/navigation and representative world proof. Rat, Roach, Troll and Wild Goblin remain staged and excluded.
