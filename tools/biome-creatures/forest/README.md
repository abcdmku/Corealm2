# Forest creature art pass

Run `node tools/biome-creatures/forest/build.mjs` from the repo root. It writes five candidate GLBs and an assetCandidates-compatible `catalog.json` to `test-results/biome-creatures/forest`. The generator does not modify the production manifest or authored world. Root acceptance and promotion are separate.

The source manifest entry is copied, including its original attribution/license records. Each output additionally records its direct source SHA-256, exact generator path, changed vertex count, removed/new triangle counts, created mesh parts, motion modifications and measured bounds. The Thorn Maw remains a derivative of its CC-BY-SA source.

Authored pieces are merged by material before export, so the Reed Strider's 24 anatomical pieces render through one mesh with three materials. Its leg tubes interpolate skin weights from one animated joint to the next, and distal tips follow the source IK foot joints. Rigid held geometry is transformed into the refitted hand's frame once. These details matter in the actual close-rig and sampled crowd paths; a floor minimum alone cannot catch detached limbs or a misplaced weapon.

Every animation is sampled against all used, fully weighted surface vertices. A separate root translation curve aligns the lowest surface to 3 mm; the report records both the original minimum and the corrected minimum. This is floor safety, not a claim that every individual foot has passed moving gameplay contact review. Root must inspect production idle/walk/run/attack/hit pictures, confirm action motion and normal combat damage before world registration.

Heartwood and chitin images were made with the builtin imagegen tool and saved in `art/biome-creatures/forest`. They are bitmap surface materials, not decals. Exact prompts are stored beside them. Runtime GLBs encode those surfaces as JPEG quality 90 with full chroma resolution; source PNGs stay intact. Their natural surface detail is shared where the material makes ecological sense; anatomy and action editing distinguish the species. Creature materials keep the production `animal_rpg_` prefix so tier tint cannot destroy the authored surface colours.

Suggested integration: Heath Jacks replace remote Fallowmarch goblin-scout placeholders; Briar Harrows replace remote forest deer/moose guardian placeholders; Fen Crawlers replace forest spiders and snails; Reed Striders occupy reeds and wet margins in place of wasps, frogs and herons; Thorn Maws replace aggressive boar/lynx/tapir beetle placeholders. Basic starter wildlife is unchanged. Existing goblin archers/shamans remain available as equipped humanoid roles.
