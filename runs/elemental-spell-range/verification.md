# Spell finales, refinement and basic tiers

2026-09-09. The lab provides four strengths of each basic spell to the sixteen existing auto-cast entries. The twenty advanced attacks remain manual in the production feature lab, with a movable action bar. Existing auto-cast progression, fuel, damage and weapon cadence are retained.

Direct projectiles aim 75% up rendered creature bounds. Area attacks stay grounded. Staff preparation uses socket glow and environmental gathering. Undertow ends with a collapsing liquid eye; Kiln Rupture replaces Phoenix Pass with seven unequal ground vents; Furnace Whip unfurls as one broad ribbon, sweeps through six contacts and sheds cinders; Sunfall has one heavy strike. Large air attacks add dark pressure, dust and fine debris. See [the art review](./art-direction-review.md).

Area attacks start contact within 1.1 seconds except Deluge at 1.21 seconds. `content/elementalTiming.ts` gives Deluge and Mountainfall more gathering time before their sharp implosions while preserving normal-speed aftermath, with the same timing used by damage, VFX and the manual cast lock. Their final inward collisions now land at 1.55 and 1.91 seconds, respectively, up from 1.12 and 1.37. Rank-five first contacts are 1.00 seconds for Skybreaker, 1.21 for Deluge, 0.88 for Mountainfall and 1.10 for Sunfall; their full durations are 4.95, 4.95, 4.41 and 4.30 seconds.

Skybreaker forms overhead and widens during descent, then releases 2,800 debris grains into varied ballistic directions. Its separate pressure pulse stays removed. Deluge borrows Undertow's open currents: the solid blue pool is removed, and the surf uses flowing teal depth, foam, refracted terrain and eroded gaps. Its wider curling waves now keep a denser lower body, with erosion concentrated at the lip. It accelerates inward and throws up torn sheets that keep weight at their bases before bending apart into falling spray. Mountainfall now uses five narrower stone anchors and 64 fracture chunks instead of eight formations and 144 chunks. Seven varied jade currents converge between the anchors, mineral light marks each contact, and four unequal energy folds surge upward at collapse. Solid chips are reduced in favor of luminous mineral particles, and released chunks crumble sooner to reveal the magic. Boulder fragments land on terrain instead of the contact-height plane, projectile wakes follow the shot's direction, and fragment rotation uses three axes. Fracture shadow coordinates and shadow fade follow the pieces; the fault ridge and collapsing outcrops use separate shader program keys.

Fire curves and folded sheets retain the dedicated flame texture and independently cropped samples. Sunfall's repeated fronts, tongues and gas pockets are now replaced by one continuous 3D combustion field. Seeded spatial noise drives density, temperature and turbulence; no flame image is projected through this volume. The impact pushes fuel outward, then rising flow breaks the boundary into uneven folds. Hot orange regions cool into red and soot. Ray integration accounts for physical thickness and uses independent sample jitter to avoid regular bands. The main blast uses one twelve-triangle proxy and a shared 256 KiB noise volume, with up to 96 GPU samples per ray. Its former repeated corona loops are one torn wake, and the ember spread no longer uses a three-lobed radial pattern. Smoke density stays inside its proxy bounds. Furnace Whip retains its 576-triangle ribbon and existing six contacts. Damage and cast timing are unchanged. See [the texture provenance](./asset-provenance.md#dedicated-flame-texture-2026-09-09).

Deluge's surrounding waves now turn inward with rising compression before the collision. The rejected flat splash fans are replaced by one turbulent refractive liquid volume and eight tapered, twisting currents. The volume rises sharply, spreads and drops; currents unravel into directional spray. Clockwise circulation is shared by the surf, collision and released droplets. A short, dense burst throws some spray sideways and some upward. The volume uses twelve proxy triangles, one shared 256 KiB spatial-noise texture and up to 56 samples per ray. It writes sampled liquid depth to retain foreground targets. The current strips retain 2,880 triangles. Foam and rain use the separate non-blooming 30,000-particle batch, with eight triangles per droplet and about 750 KiB of cached launch data. The new volume is included in principal-body counts and clears on reset. Timing, damage and the other elements are unchanged.

Sunfall keeps its continuous combustion field, but its explosion extinction factor is reduced from 1.4 to 0.6. A warped spatial mask now creates clear gaps through the gas instead of filling the whole footprint. Its incoming sun and the smaller fire spells retain their previous density. The single impact, flame colors and cooling aftermath remain.

## Action bars, spellbook and runes (2026-09-09, later pass)

The manual bar is replaced by production action bars sized from the Knight Online bar: eight square slots per bar, up to four bars, one by default in the world and three in the range. Bars dock bottom, left or right, stack in bar order, or sit where the grip dropped them; a `⋯` menu flips orientation, locks slots and shows or hides bars. Slots bind by drag from the spellbook, swap between slots, clear on right-click or when dragged off. Keys are the digit row (bar one), Shift+digit (bar two) and Alt+digit (bar three) through the shared key registry, so the Controls panel lists them and an open conversation keeps the number row.

Every spell has an authored 32 px icon (`ui/spellIcons.ts`): twenty invocation motifs and four basic motifs drawn at four sizes with rung pips. Icons use the element's render palette.

The twenty invocations are registered in the world (`content/spells.ts` `ADVANCED_SPELLS`, 36 spells total). Each spends its Essence plus a rank rune; ranks two to five strike an area and also spend a Field Rune. Six rune items (`focus_rune`, `sweep_rune`, `binding_rune`, `siege_rune`, `cataclysm_rune`, `field_rune`) are sold at the region general stores and shown on the spellbook's rune shelf with carried counts. The spellbook filters Basic or All. Automatic selection and the standing spell only ever use basics; invocations are cast once from a slot or a tile. Targeted invocations fire at the current target on the next cast beat; area invocations are placed through a ground reticle (`render/aimReticle.ts`) that turns red past 15 m and are cast at once with `GameApi.castArea`. Damage uses the lab's pulse plans: the rolled max hit is shared across the pulses that reach the aim point, each nearby enemy is rolled separately, and area pulses re-check that a creature is still inside when they land. The caster is locked for the choreography (up to 4.95 s for the finales after the timing refinement) and the lock is shown on the bar.

Checks: `tests/advanced-spells.test.ts` (36 spells, rune costs by rank, area flags against the pulse plans, rune block and spend, unchanged basic spend, 36 distinct icons), the existing spell suites, and the two browser gates named in the feature-lab workflow. Screenshots land in ignored `test-results/elemental-spells/action-bar/` and `world-bar/`.
