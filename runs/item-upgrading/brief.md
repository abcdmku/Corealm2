# Item upgrading

Add ranks +1 through +10 to armor, weapons and jewelry. Monster equipment drops stop at +7. Monster level and regional tier raise typical drop rank, including stronger versions of lower-tier equipment; low-level monsters retain extremely rare +5 through +7 outcomes.

Use `C:/Users/Borg/Downloads/stone+circular+platform+3d+model.glb` for an upgrade fount in the tier-one town center. Remove unwanted bottom geometry while preserving the supplied source.

Weapons and armor use low, medium and high upgrade scrolls. Minibosses drop scrolls much more often than regular monsters, with low-level minibosses favoring basic scrolls and rarely yielding higher scrolls. Jewelry combines three identical same-rank pieces into one next-rank piece.

Separate magic enhancements use rare materials from high-level monsters. Armor supports bonuses such as strength, health and recoil; weapons support poison damage, flame damage and slowing monsters. Bosses very rarely drop a Trina-like chance booster, also sold for 100,000,000 gold.

Match the published Knight Online upgrade rates where specified: https://knight-online.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrading. Weapons at +8 and higher show particles. Armor at +8 pulses faintly, +9 glows brightly and +10 is radiant. Clarify missing or conflicting rules before implementation.
