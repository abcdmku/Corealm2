# Existing equipment texture revision

The owner stopped the procedural armor rebuild. The current authorized scope is to use the established armor and weapon models, remove scarves from melee armor, and replace the textures of melee armor and all weapons with detailed surfaces based on the approved item icons.

No new armor geometry is authorized. Magic armor receives no texture changes. Existing mesh positions, topology, UVs, skeletons, weights, and animations must be preserved. Scarf removal uses the existing separate Knight scarf parts.

Astra medium authors implement the texture pipeline. Fresh Luna max critics inspect actual in-game captures against the icon material references. Root owns contracts, candidate integration, lab proof, and final-world acceptance. Only accepted texture candidates may replace production textures.

Cleanup: removed 40 rejected armor manifest entries and 61 rejected registry records. Physical deletion of abandoned core/builders/candidates/evidence is blocked by automatic approval policy, including a separately checked single core directory. Files remain inactive pending permitted cleanup. Approved icons and unrelated accepted item assets are preserved.
