# Shared armor core revision

The owner rejected the independent armor builders for bulk, basic materials, and body penetration. No rejected armor is accepted by this revision.

The replacement architecture has two native-body fitted bases: plate and tailored hide. All tier wrappers share those fit surfaces. Material color, subtle edging, and small fasteners distinguish tiers. The approved item icons remain the design references.

## Texture sources

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Originals remain in the Codex generated-images directory. Workspace copies are in `art/item-models/textures`.

- `worked-steel-albedo.png`: flat tileable worked steel, restrained fine hammering and scratches, uniform diffuse albedo, no lighting or objects. Original `exec-2dde5333-bafe-40e5-9f24-f6a8a9839a26.png`.
- `tanned-leather-albedo.png`: flat tileable vegetable-tanned brown leather, fine supple grain, no large cracks, stitching, objects, or directional lighting. Original `exec-65c15e1f-2b8f-470c-b7c4-49655e9d8080.png`.
- `neutral-leather-albedo.png`: reference edit preserving that grain and layout while removing brown pigment for material tinting. Original `exec-d16f6c6c-fbe0-4928-adfd-feebf492105d.png`.

UVs use a 0.25 m tile. Fine normal and roughness maps provide restrained surface response. The export preserves texture sampling and PBR parameters.

## Current acceptance

Corven and Marchhide are the only new core candidates. Initial worn captures were rejected by root and a fresh Luna max critic. Work continues on joint coverage, hood construction, readable seams, and robe movement. No core model has been promoted.

The candidate source fingerprint includes the wrapper, imported shared core, profile, native body GLB, exporter, skin adapter, and albedo files. Any change invalidates prior evidence for the affected core.
