# Corealm content rebuild specification

Status: Approved for implementation by the owner's explicit September 4 rebuild instruction. Existing gameplay rules remain authoritative except where this amendment changes world interaction and presentation. Root owns contracts and integration.

The owner's later visual correction supersedes the earlier sparse low-poly interpretation. Retain a stylized identity with finer botanical silhouettes, curved branches and leaves, weathered rock contours and detailed material surfaces. Large flat facets, chunky leaf clusters, flat colour plus generic noise, and polygon-count reduction alone do not meet the requested quality. Judge close views and normal play views before expanding a representative asset treatment across its family.

The owner subsequently identified the blocky distance substitutions and explicitly rejected them. Keep the detailed source model at every visible distance. Remove the simplified foliage assets and their runtime transition path. Retain ordinary instancing, spatial/frustum culling and continuous animation evaluation. Verify the actual production foliage path while moving away and returning; model-gallery screenshots do not prove this behavior.

The owner's subsequent instruction explicitly extends delivery to the entire game, including structures, equipment and creatures, and at least 24 new distinct creature species with complete animations, useful loot tables and authored world placement. Recolors, scale changes and tier duplicates do not count as new species. Audit every current asset family and gameplay system; resolve concrete shipping defects, review every new creature in the production lab, and verify its encounter and loot before world integration. Earlier partial acceptance does not complete this scope.

## Required changes

1. Inventory all 273 imported models by family, geometry, textures and clips. Record retain, repair or replace decisions with concrete reasons. Do not call a shader grade a model replacement.
2. Build an original, consistent environment set for trees, understory and geology. Use anatomically organized branching, grounded roots, species-specific crowns, restrained material variation, layered rock formations and integrated mineral seams. Eliminate the repeated oversized leaf cards and toy-like ore boulders from production paths after lab acceptance.
3. Replace generic resource spirals with authored local layouts. Mines need enclosing geology, exposed worked faces and accessible work floors. Groves, fisheries, creature habitats and settlement approaches need their own composition rules. Preserve important route relationships and existing resource IDs while rebuilding local placement and terrain.
4. Make ordinary visible forest trees harvestable within playable bounds. Use stable descriptors and nearby semantic activation so forest interaction does not create thousands of permanent animated entities. Depletion, respawn and save/load must agree with the displayed tree.
5. Remove the hard animation freeze/snap transition. Keep distance updates continuous and preserve motion phase across representation changes. Give missing creature hit reactions an actual articulated response and attacks species-appropriate anticipation, contact and recovery. Remove sliding root-lunge motion where it conflicts with planted feet.
6. Repair false-green verification. Support explicit semantic expectations and fail on game errors. Reuse a live development server and browser for iterative probes. Prune tests that only copy presentation constants or inspect shader text when real visual fixtures provide the relevant proof. Keep state, persistence, collision and deterministic generation coverage.
7. Measure complete-scene performance, including resident scenery and animation. Reduce unnecessary submissions, asset duplication and scheduling work without hiding missing content behind a lighter benchmark.

## Work and acceptance

Boot the game and production lab first, freeze shared interfaces, then give workers separate file ownership. New models and reusable site compositions enter a deterministic lab fixture before world integration. Root checks source, real input, semantic changes, motion over time and screenshots. Fresh critics review results without editing.

World layout, site relief, scatter composition and long-distance tree residency have a narrow full-world exception under `docs/world-authoring.md`, because their spatial integration is the behavior under test. Their reusable models, local interactions and animation still require lab proof. Terrain remains the single source for rendering, physics, placement and navigation. Regenerate dependent navigation/map artifacts when their input changes.

Acceptance requires meaningful visual improvement at gameplay and close distances, catalogue-wide disposition, production model usage, harvest/deplete/respawn behavior, motion continuity, truthful scenario verdicts, builds and focused correctness checks. Measure the full-world GPU budget and report any unresolved failure. Do not substitute a test count for visual quality or call an incomplete rebuild a finished AAA release.
