# Slice 10: exterior geology

The two authored agility shortcuts, `corealm_sunder_ledge` and `corealm_scree_slide`, are regenerated from
`tools/build-corealm-geology.ts`, accepted in the production environment lab and in the world, and promoted.
The geology provenance failure that blocked `npm run build` for the whole project is resolved, and the build
now succeeds in this worktree.

## The build blocker was a stale CRLF checkout

Main declared six original generator entrypoints `text eol=lf` in `.gitattributes` so their provenance hashes
match on Windows and CI, and pinned their LF hashes in `game/public/assets/manifest.json`. Git does not
renormalise files already in the working tree, so this worktree still had CRLF copies of all six and every
pinned hash missed its own source. `npx tsx tools/validate-game-content.ts` reported eighteen problems across
six packs: `corealm-original-geology`, `corealm-creature-expansion`, `corealm-original-farm`,
`corealm-original-minerals`, `corealm-original-ground-ores` and `corealm-original-equipment`. Only
`corealm-original-nature` passed, because main had rewritten that file after the rule landed.

Deleting and re-checking out those six files cleared all eighteen. The recorded hashes were already correct;
no hash was copied anywhere. `tools/build-bedded-shortcuts.ts` had the same problem against its own staged
catalogue, and the four staged shortcut generators now carry the same `eol=lf` rule.

This means five of the six pack failures were never geology's, and the "three original-generator mismatches"
in `finish-release-plan.md` are a checkout artifact rather than three unreconciled outputs. The remaining
genuine geology work was that the served shortcut GLBs predated an earlier generator edit.

## Regenerate and accept, then promote

The served `corealm_sunder_ledge` and `corealm_scree_slide` bytes were generated before commit `a71832d`
changed the shortcut fields, so `tools/build-corealm-geology.ts` no longer reproduced them. Every other asset
in both `corealm-original-geology` and `corealm-original-ground-ores` still regenerated byte for byte,
including all twelve Slice 11 ground ores; nothing in the ore generator is mismatched.

The generator's shortcut formation was then reworked and the output regenerated, inspected and promoted. No
hash was written without regenerating and accepting its output.

(`test-results/geo-gallery/r1`):

| --- | --- |

| Long runs of identical ~0.16 m marching-cubes steps where hard fracture cuts grazed the surface | Cut planes are subtracted with a rounded `smoothMax` and displaced by a continuous metre-scale wave up to 6 cm, so their intersection curves no longer follow grid rows; resolution went 64 to 76, not higher |

Views inspected for both formations: primary framed, opposite, side, approach at eye height, and gameplay
distance. Accepted captures are in `test-results/geo-gallery/r4`; the pre-change baseline is retained in

Held: a short staircase remains on the inside wall of Sunder's central cleft, deep in shadow and not visible

## Provenance

`corealm-original-geology` is fully procedural. Its GLBs contain no image textures, only positions, normals,
vertex colours and metre-density UVs, and the renderer supplies the shared stone maps at runtime. Nothing in
the pack derives from DEXSOFT, so no DEXSOFT provenance was recorded on it.

The DEXSOFT chain was checked rather than assumed, for the packs that do derive from it.
`tools/import-unity-magic-assets.ps1` refuses to extract unless `Rocks FREE pack.unitypackage` hashes to
`a81e0968a134f1720b028a534634377784a84f72294a95590b8361a8d176f5d2`, which is the value recorded in
`finish-release-plan.md`, and `tools/import-unity-magic-assets.ts` pins `rocks_free_essence_node` at
`c1c3c2af9eaed4027d80c84ed64422c9fb261eabc8bc275334a6a834fb541a1d`. The served
`game/public/assets/models/magic/rocks_free_essence_node.glb` hashes to exactly that, and
`corealm-original-ground-ores` already carries it as its `sourceReference`. The archive hash is therefore
verified provenance and could be added to the `dexsoft-rocks-free` manifest pack, but that pack belongs to
another owner and the manifest may only be written by the promotion helper, so it is left recorded here.
