# Corealm production polish brief

The owner requested an overhaul on September 4, 2026. The current game is close in overall look and feel, but foliage, creatures, items and other presentation do not feel unified or refined. The requested quality ambition is a production-ready AAA game.

Preserve the existing stylized fantasy RPG identity and its working progression. Apply the existing approved Corealm art requirements to the current game. Establish measurable visual and interaction standards, implement them in the production feature lab, then check the authored world. A visual pass alone cannot establish AAA production readiness.

The owner confirmed the direction during the run: "Keep it, with cohesive art and much stronger polish."
