# Deep Wilderness expansion

The user's September 10 request authorizes implementation of this amendment to `runs/corealm/PRD.md`. Both the current game and the production environment lab booted in Chromium after magic commit `5a8bdfb`, with ready state and no browser or game errors.

The Wilderness extends from z=460 to z=940, twice its previous depth. The southern half is T50; the northern half is T70. `content/wildernessDepth.ts` freezes these bands, the smooth visual transition, five rune keeper identities and shared structure/resource reservations. Semantic RegionId and saved region ownership remain unchanged. `contracts.ts` and `app/worldSpec.ts` are root-owned.

Mind and Chaos keepers occupy the shallow half. Death, Blood and Wrath keepers occupy the deep half. All use the merged spell inventory rune IDs. Cosmic Runes accompany area-spell supply. Ordinary packs contain 7–15 residents; bosses remain individual actors. Every regional miniboss targets 3–5 times its region's tier in the existing computed combat-level formula. Authored stat blocks, not a cosmetic label, must produce those levels.

Shallow art uses cracked rock with orange molten interiors, scorched but partly living groves, and red, black and lava hatchlings. Deep art uses blue and violet fissures, restrained drifting particles, magic trees and full red, black and purple dragons. New boss silhouettes must show structural differences in bodies and movement. Surface glitter or scale alone is insufficient.

Reusable actors, trees, structures, lava/pool effects and resource interactions require production lab state, real input and inspected images before final-world registration. The final world exception applies only to the northward extension, terrain relief, continuous biome/sky composition, scatter, road/river/structure layout, coastal continuation and navigation. Those changes require final-world generation and placement proof because a compact fixture cannot exercise their spatial relationships.

Root owns shared contracts, registration, architecture, integration, final navigation/map generation and combined acceptance. Concurrent workers receive exclusive modules, generator directories, asset staging directories and focused tests. They do not edit public manifests or promote art before root acceptance.

Acceptance will use focused bounded lab matrices before final-world checks. Browser/GPU jobs are serialized; whole-game and combined lab checks run only at root. Durable user assets and source are retained; generated review evidence stays in ignored `test-results` unless explicitly promoted.
