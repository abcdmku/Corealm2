# Deep Wilderness lava

The expansion has three branching river systems, a new fork on Widow's Furnace, four connected basins and nine scattered pools. Pool positions and shore shapes use independent fixed seeds. `WILDERNESS_LAVA_EXPANSION_CHANNELS` remains separate from active world registration until root accepts the lab fixture.

Every channel and pool uses the same curved spine and width sampler for terrain carving, molten rendering, bank clearance and collision capsules. Pools use broad, rounded cross-sections. Connected ends remain open beneath the adjoining flow. Banks and loose bank rocks stop at adjoining molten ground, so a fork does not gain a dry wall across its mouth. A thin dry cinder apron covers the bank width and a 0.65 m outer fringe, joining the berms and rounded end caps. It follows the existing ground with adaptive subdivision at steep contacts and does not change terrain, molten ground or collision.

The production depth field sets colour at each molten vertex. Warm red and orange fissures become violet with blue hot cores farther north. Narrow, interrupted molten openings show between dark torn slag slabs. Each slab has a ragged rim, raised uneven interior, and production stone normal and roughness maps. Sparks and nearby bank light follow the molten colour. All channels and torches share six fixed point lights, assigned to the nearest sources. Unused lights retain their visibility with zero intensity to avoid shader variants during travel.

`DEEP_WILDERNESS_LAVA_LAB_CHANNELS` contains a warm fork, tributary, connected broad basin and a separate deep pool. `deepWildernessEffectsLabTorches()` includes ordinary braziers and one violet ward. The fixture runs through the normal terrain, renderer, particles, physics and player movement paths at `?mode=combat&atmosphere=1&wildernessEffects=deep`.

Run focused tests with `npx vitest run tests/deep-wilderness-lava.test.ts tests/wilderness-effects.test.ts`. The browser driver is `npx tsx tools/deep-wilderness-lava-lab-test.ts`, with optional `--url`. It uses a 60-second budget and normal gameplay camera focus, pitch and zoom. It records warm and deep views, animation state, real dry-bank movement and the same held movement stopping at molten ground. Evidence goes to ignored `test-results/deep-wilderness-lava-lab` and requires image review.

The authored full-world exception applies only to river and pool placement relative to the expanded terrain, roads, habitats, structures, resources and coast. Final integration must use the accepted expansion constant for every terrain, render, scatter-clearance, navigation and movement caller, then rebuild navigation and the world map. Focused tests reserve all three frozen structure footprints and six resource intentions. The population layout worker checks complete moving pack footprints against the same proposed channels.

The first Chromium run passed semantic checks in 15.6 seconds with no console or page errors. Image review rejected broad soft emission, a flat printed appearance and small triangular bank gaps. The revision adds 73 raised slabs to the fixture, narrows the hot openings, lowers deep peak emission and clips banks to the curved shore. A 15.96-second retake used player-follow cameras at 11 m and recorded 8.34 m of dry walking versus 3.332 m before stopping at molten ground. The critic accepted the material revision and requested continuous dry bank closure. The cinder apron addresses that request, with downward-ray checks of its bank and end-cap contact. All nine focused tests pass. The apron still needs a fresh image retake before root acceptance.
