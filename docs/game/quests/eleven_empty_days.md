Trapper Mott has eleven traps in the deep wood. In eleven days they have caught eleven nothings. He would like a second opinion, and he would like it delivered gently.

![Trapper Mott](../assets/captures/npcs/npc_trapper_mott.webp)

| Giver | Start location | Region | Requirements | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Trapper Mott](../../npcs/#trapper-mott) | [Oakwood](../../regions/#oakwood) | [Woodlands](../../regions/#woodlands) | None | None |



## Walkthrough

### 1. Walk Mott's trap line: the Blackwater Pools, the Gorge Head, The Thicket Camp and the Gorge Ford, in any order.

All four are route-graph nodes: `moveTo({ locationId })` reaches each one directly. The Thicket is where Thorn Maws keep to the edge, so go there with health to spare or take the long way round by the ford.

### 3. Report to Trapper Mott in Oakwood. Decide on the way whether to mention the thing you noticed about how his traps are set.

Both answers finish the quest. One of them changes what Mott says to you for the rest of the game, and it is not the kind one.
