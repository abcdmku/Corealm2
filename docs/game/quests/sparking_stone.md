Quarrier Vess has been cutting Cobalt for nine years and she has never liked what it does in the dark. She would like somebody who is not her to find out what is in it.

![Quarrier Vess](../assets/captures/npcs/npc_quarrier_vess.webp)

| Giver | Start location | Region | Requirements | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Quarrier Vess](../../npcs/#quarrier-vess) | [Hillcrest](../../regions/#hillcrest) | [Highlands](../../regions/#highlands) | Mining 10 | None |

## Supplied when accepted

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| [Pine Log](../../items/#pine-log) | 1 |
| [Air Essence](../../items/#air-essence) | 100 |
| Unlock | Vess gives you one pine log and 100 measures of Air Essence. |

## Walkthrough

### 1. Return to Farmland and kill the Storm Scarab west of the Air Essence Cache.

Go south through Woodlands to Millfield, then follow the western track to locationId fallowmarch_air_cache. The Storm Scarab, entity tempest_roc, roams about 42 metres west of the cache. The Air Essence Vess gave you can power Voltrend during the fight.

### 2. Loot the Air Orb dropped by the Storm Scarab.

The guaranteed Air Orb remains in the Storm Scarab's loot pile after the kill. Use `loot` on that pile. If you already used its Orb to awaken the Air Altar, that earlier awakening counts and you can continue with your staff.

### 3. Make an Air Staff at the Air Altar, then equip it.

If the Air Altar is dormant, awaken it with the Air Orb first. At a fletching bench, make an Pine Shaft, then an Pine Staff. Make an Air Staff at the awakened altar and equip it. Once partly spent, the same altar restores it to 1000 for exactly 100 Air Essence.

### 4. Raise Magic to level 5 by casting Voltrend at something that will hold still for it.

The Air Staff spends its charge before carried Air Essence. Frogs near locationId redsill_shallows are cheap targets. Recharge at entity fallowmarch_air_altar with 100 Air Essence when needed.
