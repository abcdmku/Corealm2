Seamer Juno makes the parts of things: shafts, cord, hide, and the elemental essence that powers magic weapons. She will teach both trades to anyone who brings her the raw material and does not pretend to already know.

<img class="codex-shot" src="../../assets/captures/npcs/npc_seamer_juno.webp" alt="Seamer Juno in the running Corealm world" width="960" height="540" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

| Giver | Start location | Region | Requirements | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Seamer Juno](../../npcs/#seamer-juno) | [Oakwood](../../regions/#oakwood) | [Woodlands](../../regions/#woodlands) | None | None |

## Supplied when accepted

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| [Quartz](../../items/pale_quartz/) | 3 |
| Unlock | Juno hands you three Quartz to start on. |

## Walkthrough

Shafts come from Pine logs, cut at the Pine Grove in Farmland (locationId `palewood_copse`). Millfield has the only fletching bench in Phase 1.
