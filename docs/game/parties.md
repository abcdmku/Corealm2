# Chat and parties

After joining a multiplayer world, chat appears in the bottom-left message log alongside quest updates and level-ups. A chat bar sits under the log. Press Enter or tap the bar to type, Enter to send, and Escape to stop typing. While you type, the log opens to show its full history.

The channel label on the left of the bar shows who hears you. Click it to switch between Say, Party and your last whisper partner. You can also type a command:

- `/s message` speaks to players within 30 metres on the same surface or dungeon floor.
- `/p message` speaks to every connected member of your party, at any distance.
- `/w name message` whispers to one online player.
- `/r message` replies to the last player who whispered you.

A command without a message switches the bar to that channel. Messages are limited to 280 characters and one per second.

The gear on the chat bar creates a party with only you in it, or leaves your current party. It also hides or shows each kind of line: game messages, warnings, nearby chat, party chat and whispers. These choices are saved in the browser.

Your party appears in the pinned quest card, below any tracked quest or hunt. Each member shows their name, combat level and a health bar. The crown marks the leader. Faded members are offline or more than 48 metres away. Right-click a member to whisper them, or to remove them if you lead. Right-click your own row to leave or disband.

To invite someone, right-click their character and choose Invite, or click + on the party card to list online players by name and level. Inviting while you have no party creates one first. Invitees see the invitation in their quest card and choose Join or ×. A party holds up to eight players, and only the leader can invite. If the leader leaves, the first remaining member becomes leader. Invitations expire after 60 seconds. A disconnected member has 30 seconds to reconnect before leaving the party automatically.

When a party member kills a monster, other living, connected members within 48 metres of the monster receive 50% of its kill-bonus XP, rounded down, in the skill that made the kill. The attacker keeps normal damage and kill XP. Spell-cast and damage XP are not shared.

Everyone nearby sees monster loot piles and can collect their contents. Picking up an item sends it directly to the next eligible member of the party that earned the pile. The order follows party join order and continues across piles. Recipients must be alive, connected, on the same floor and within 48 metres of the pile. Full inventories and already-owned unique orbs are skipped. If nobody can receive an item, it stays in the pile. A partially collected stack leaves its remaining quantity behind.

Solo drops, and drops whose original party has disbanded, use the collector's party or go to the collector when solo. Currency keeps the normal direct kill reward. Recovery caches remain private.

Party membership, the next loot recipient and collected items persist with the world. Chat and invitations do not survive a server restart. Hosts and clients require multiplayer protocol version 3.
