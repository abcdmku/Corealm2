# Wilderness dragons

Six production candidates use three separate licensed Dungeon Mason winged dragon rigs. The model bases are Terror Bringer for red dragons, Usurper for black dragons and Soul Eater for the lava hatchling and purple adult. The verified archive is the owner's local `Dragon for Boss Monster PBR.unitypackage`, SHA-256 `01b15c6e6ac1339acc40e653924691583cbf44303397878ae7f79a59112b7383`, under the Standard Unity Asset Store EULA. The existing manifest pack is `dungeon-mason-four-evil-dragons-pbr`.

The shallow species are `baby_red_dragon`, `baby_black_dragon` and `baby_lava_dragon`, all T50. The deep species are `red_wilderness_dragon`, `black_wilderness_dragon` and `purple_wilderness_dragon`, all T70. Their public asset IDs add the `creature_` prefix. Each proposed production file is `game/public/assets/models/creature/<asset ID>.glb`. The staging catalog is `test-results/wilderness-dragons/catalog.json`; no generator promotes its own work.

The shared combat-level tuner produces base levels 50, 53 and 56 for red, black and lava hatchlings, and 72, 75 and 78 for red, black and purple adults. These levels come from the actual offence, defence and health values. Species movement, attack cadence and native body scale stay unchanged. Final encounter progression can add its small depth adjustment after lab acceptance.

Hatchlings have a broader brow and abdomen, shortened neck and tail, smaller wing fingers and a faster gait with small head and tail adjustments. The deformation changes vertices, skeleton rest translations, inverse binds and animation translation keys together. It retains all three source rigs' original skin weights and separate wing membranes. Black adults keep the low four-legged stalk and elongated skull. Red adults have the raised wyvern chest and hooked wing claws. Purple adults have broader folded wing shoulders and the Soul Eater's plated throat.

The source UV islands, 2048px albedo detail and tangent normal maps remain intact. Normals and occlusion use 1024px maps at gameplay scale. The red lineage's ochre source skin has a masked burgundy midtone grade, with pale membranes and claws protected by the luminance mask. This keeps the source scale grain while making the skin's red identity clearer at night. The lava hatchling uses warm basalt grey midtones and the purple adult uses muted violet slate. Their colour grade lifts the native dark skin texture without changing its UV detail, normals or occlusion. Both use the full diffuse factor. Lava and purple fissures use a few open branching paths around the throat, shoulders and flanks, baked into the native UV islands. Their asymmetric branches taper to dark ends. They do not close into cells or cover the body with an edge network. Emissive strength is reduced so that the fissures do not overwhelm the body material. Most of each body remains opaque scale material.

`python tools/wilderness-dragons/extract.py` checks the archive hash and extracts only the relevant sources into ignored staging. `node tools/wilderness-dragons/build.mjs` builds all six candidates. `--only <species ID>` rebuilds one. The generator binds FBX animation channels by Model ID and complete ancestry, including repeated left/right wing names. It fixes horizontal root translation and samples skinned geometry at 60 Hz to author 3 mm floor clearance. The red source's airborne Run is replaced with its faster planted Walk for a grounded chase. The other lineages retain their native Run. All candidates retain Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Hit, Death and a source Breath clip. Breath is available animation art, not an additional combat damage mechanic.

The initial CPU acceptance passed `npx vitest run tests/wilderness-dragons.test.ts`. It verifies native UVs, three wing skeletons, normalized skin weights, juvenile proportions, archive provenance and sparse emission. It also loads the exported GLBs through Three's real GLTF loader and samples interpolated poses between the generator's floor keys across every clip, including death. These are geometry checks; browser art acceptance remains the root's responsibility.

`npx tsx tools/wilderness-dragons/lab-test.ts` checks the shallow trio; `--deep` checks the adult trio. The driver uses the production creature gallery, real animation controls, semantic motion and drawn bounds, screenshots and a real-input fight. It selects `wilderness` or `deep_wilderness` and waits until the sky's night blend exceeds .98. Adult captures also require the magic blend above .98; shallow captures require it below .02. `wildernessCreatures=1` enables the production creature particles, whose live emitter identity and ember/arcane palette are checked before capture. Attack starts from Idle and runs without another authoring control until natural Idle recovery. Hit then asserts the actual masked overlay on the idle rig. Explicit gallery locomotion controls intentionally override active actions, so the driver never presses Idle midway through Attack. Camera poses follow the player at ground height, with 8 m or 11 m interactive zoom and legal pitch. They do not detach focus or raise the target. Run the two matrices serially in an assigned GPU lane. The root must inspect the frames, accept the candidates and only then promote them and register authored encounters.

At the current placement-proposal checkpoint, shallow nests contain 9 red, 8 black and 9 lava hatchlings, for 26 hatchlings total. The deep proposal has two seven-red packs, one seven-black pack and one seven-purple pack, for 28 adults total. These proposals meet the 7–15 pack requirement. Source-space scalars are .25 red hatchling, .25 black hatchling, .32 lava hatchling, .59 red adult, .51 black adult and .75 purple adult. They are baked into authored geometry. Species scale is `1 / tierSilhouetteScale(tier)`, so the renderer draws these measured metre dimensions without adding the T50/T70 silhouette enlargement.

Promotion needs no new provenance allowlist or pack. Every asset references the existing licensed `dungeon-mason-four-evil-dragons-pbr` pack. Its HTTP Asset Store source and Standard Unity Asset Store EULA already pass both production manifest provenance and docs validation. The added authoring script, archive hash and modifications remain in per-asset metadata.

`assets/art/wilderness-dragons/source-and-measurements.json` records each export hash, source mesh and texture paths, dimensions, per-clip extrema and measured stance speeds. Ordinary locomotion envelopes include the tail and wings. Placement should keep about 12 m between adult centres and give dying dragons additional clearance from walls, since their wing collapse extends beyond the walking envelope.

The first hardware Chromium matrices passed in 20.7 seconds shallow and 20.8 seconds deep. They reported no browser or game errors. A real player attack reduced red hatchling health from 135 to 129 and purple adult health from 212 to 206. Each lineage used a native masked hit overlay after its attack completed. Review rejected the original two emitting masks because their regular cells read as a grid. The replacement irregular fissures passed focused browser retakes in 14.667 seconds shallow and 14.889 seconds deep, with live regional particles and no errors. The corresponding historical reports are `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-shallow-fissures/report.json` and `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-deep-fissures/report.json`. Review of their idle and side-walk screenshots then held the nearly black body material. The basalt/slate repair supersedes those emitting asset hashes and requires full-cycle browser retakes.

The focused CPU material audit is `test-results/wilderness-dragons/stone-material-audit.json`. Both emitting albedos retain 2048 by 2048 pixels. Their normal and AO bytes exactly match the original conversion. Median albedo luminance is .409 for lava and .383 for purple, while the 10th percentile remains .292 and .299 respectively. The subsequent open-fault mask audit is `test-results/wilderness-dragons/fault-material-audit.json`. Current fissures cover about 1.00 percent of occupied lava UV texels and .39 percent of purple UV texels, leaving broad native scale areas uninterrupted. The geometry, UV, native animation and grounding tests pass after the material repair. These CPU measurements do not replace review under settled regional lighting.

Review accepted the dragon anatomy but held the red lineage's near-black night colour and the short browser motion sampling. The red albedo grade now requires new browser art review. The driver has `--red-only`, `--black-only` and `--emitting-only` filters, each optionally paired with `--deep`. The revised reports use a `-full` directory suffix so that previous evidence is preserved. An independent in-page frame sampler records motion every 25 milliseconds or the next rendered frame, so PNG encoding cannot skip phases. Walk and Run each record at least 1.05 entire clip cycles, including a phase wrap. Attack starts from Idle, records the protected one-shot through its natural Idle recovery, then Hit tests the native masked recoil in isolation. Each motion includes beginning, middle and late screenshots, with recovery screenshots for Attack and Hit.

These browser gallery checks inspect complete stationary gait cycles, body grounding and animation transitions. They do not establish that the feet match a travelling world creature's exact speed. The real-input combat check proves incoming damage to the dragon. It does not establish outgoing dragon attack/contact synchronization. `contactNormalized` in the export metadata estimates maximum forward head reach in the native clip; it is not measured combat damage timing.

Every dragon lab invocation explicitly uses `startGameServer({hmr:false})` and `installTestDeadline(..., 60_000)`. The deadline includes owned browser and server cleanup. Focused family retakes remain separate jobs within that same budget.

The final all-six motion batches passed in 33.145 seconds shallow and 41.051 seconds deep, with no browser or game errors. Reports are `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-shallow-full/report.json` and `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-deep-full/report.json`. The fresh read-only critic accepted red/black materials and visible anatomy and confirmed full gait cycles, natural Attack recovery and isolated native Hit overlays for all six. Adult red/black still need an unobstructed side view because the panel hides part of a wing in frontal frames. The critic held the emitting assets for their complete-cell crack topology. Current open branching masks supersede those two report hashes; their geometry, clips and diffuse grades are unchanged. Run `--emitting-only --material-only` shallow and `--deep --material-only` for the short material and adult silhouette retakes. These jobs inspect idle/walk key poses, live particles and a legal side view without repeating combat checks.

The final material retakes passed in 12.926 seconds shallow and 23.471 seconds deep. Current reports are `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-shallow-fissures-materials/report.json` and `test-results/wilderness-dragons/lab-deep-materials/report.json`. The fresh read-only dragon critic cleared both emitting material holds: the body now carries the form between a few localized open fissures, with no closed crack network or glitter treatment. New adult red/black side views show wing contours and tails clear of the gallery panel and close the silhouette gap. The lava side view still has some player overlap at the flank and tail root; frontal key poses establish its accepted material readability. The full-cycle reports retain their geometry and motion scope, while their previous lava/purple material screenshots are superseded. `test-results/wilderness-dragons/final-proof-index.json` maps every final asset hash to its current material evidence and full-motion report. All six candidates are ready for root acceptance and promotion; the worker has not promoted them.
