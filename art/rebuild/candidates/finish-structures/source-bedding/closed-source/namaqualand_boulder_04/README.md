# Selected thick source: Namaqualand Boulder 04

[Namaqualand Boulder 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_boulder_04), by Jenelle van Heerden / Poly Haven, is a CC0 asset under the [official asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Official API metadata, original geometry and verified download hashes are retained here. Maps have not been downloaded and no derivative has been made.

The un-tilted source measures 2.522 × 1.900 × 2.496 m. Height divided by the longest horizontal dimension is 0.7535, exceeding the required 0.55. It has 59,066 triangles, one connected component, zero welded boundary/nonmanifold edges, zero indexed degeneracies, Euler characteristic 2 and positive signed volume. These checks do not establish exhaustive triangle self-intersection freedom.

I inspected front.png, rear.png and side.png, generated locally from untouched source triangles. It is one thick angular mass with rough fractured faces and a complete back. Its native base is broad and its upper silhouette is uneven. It does not require the major pitch that made Rock 09 unsupported. Its bedding is less pronounced than Rock Face 01, but its complete body and native thickness are a better match for a freestanding cliff mass.

The unrotated axis fits and comparison with both legacy bounds are in comparison.json. Scree requires only about 1.09 maximum/minimum axis distortion. Sunder requires about 1.58, primarily horizontal widening and height reduction. These are measurements only; there is no adapted asset or claim of accepted world grounding.

The bounded search screened Rock 07, Boulder 01 and Namaqualand Boulder 03 before selecting this source. Their height/longest-horizontal ratios were approximately 0.449, 0.548 and 0.481, respectively. They remain geometry-only records and were not selected. Earlier Rock 09 and panel failures remain preserved.
