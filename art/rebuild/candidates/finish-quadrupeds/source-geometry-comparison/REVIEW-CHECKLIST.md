# Frozen geometry comparison

This batch contains 12 production gallery views, four each for Fox, Badger and Monitor: front, side, rear and gameplay. Hardware proof completed at `test-results/quadruped-source-geometry-comparison/`. All 12 PNGs were personally inspected. The report confirms NVIDIA RTX 5080 Direct3D11, zero browser errors, no missing assets and exact candidate hashes. The browser closed and GPU lease was released before image review. Candidate bytes are copied into this folder; `catalogue.json` records full asset and source-catalogue hashes.

No public assets change in this batch. Root owns final acceptance and promotion.

## Results and next repairs

Root reviewed Fox side, Badger front/side and Monitor front/side/rear. Monitor's static direction was accepted, with natural lifecycle still pending. The remaining angles preserve the continuous tapered tail and readable monitor silhouette. Static images cannot establish planted feet during travel or turns.

Fox's continuous body, smoother silhouette and coat are improved. Its feet still end in narrow wedges rather than convincing paws, especially in front and side views. Root requested a substantive distal source-mesh paw repair. A separate motion repair reaches small sampled contact errors but lowers the pelvis by up to 53 mm in Walk and 139 mm in Run; that pose change requires visual review, and the new paws must be measured again.

Badger's complete source body is useful, with short legs and identifiable head markings. Its converted material is near-black and excessively glossy with gold reflections, concealing the body. Root requested original shader/channel interpretation and roughness correction before judging the coat. Original creator rights remain provisional; no deep derivative is authorized yet.
