Original: [Sheep, rigged, textured and animated](https://opengameart.org/content/sheep-rigged-textured-and-animated). Texture photograph source: [Woodland animals texture pack](https://opengameart.org/content/woodland-animals-texture-pack). The original downloaded Blender file remains unchanged in the adjacent read-only `source-caprine-free` package; its SHA-256 and derivative hashes are in `asset-ledger.json`.

The candidate preserves the complete selected Sheep 2 body: all 1,470 source vertices, 1,522 polygons, original UVs and named vertex weights before one interpolating subdivision. A continuous spatial deformation narrows the face and torso, broadens the neck/chest, raises the belly and changes the skull profile; bone rest landmarks follow the same deformation. No replacement torso, muzzle, leg, eye or separate black hoof block is grafted onto it. Regional coat colors darken the hooves and neck and lighten the rump/muzzle. Two continuous, closed, tapered horns use an asymmetric rounded triangular section with shallow growth grooves and bind to the original HEAD bone. Original proportions and hoof topology remain recognizable; visual acceptance is still required.

Anatomy references: [NPS Rocky Mountain bighorn](https://www.nps.gov/romo/learn/nature/bighorn_sheep.htm) and [NPS Sheep 101](https://www.nps.gov/features/colm/virtualtour/section/hard/activity/sheep-101/). Local reference images are for inspection, not runtime textures. The compact open horn curl and broad neck/chest follow those references; this is not a measured anatomical reconstruction.

Run `node tools/creature-expansion/hoofed/source-bighorn-sheep.mjs` from the repository root. It uses the existing Blender runtime with auto-execution disabled, then verifies the exported meshes, normalized weights, 22-joint skin, embedded texture and coat attributes. Outputs remain in this directory.

`review-catalogue.json` points to a rest-pose candidate for the parent's scheduled production viewer review. It is not promoted or accepted. `bighorn-source-adaptation.blend` preserves the editable full mesh/rig and seven genuine actions. `bighorn-source-adaptation.glb` includes those actions under their `Source_*` names. All sampled vertices are finite, but source motion contact fails: the unmodified source action rotations do not preserve planted feet after the anatomical changes. Numerical results, including sole positions, are recorded in `adaptation-report.json`. Ground offsets are not concealed by per-frame root correction.

There is no authored Run, Attack, Hit, HitLeft, HitRight or Death. None is aliased to a source action. Source_idle and Source_walking are also not declared production-ready. Animation repair, contact and deformation review, lifecycle roles, and GPU screenshot acceptance remain necessary before integration. No production modules, catalogue or frozen source files are edited by this builder.
