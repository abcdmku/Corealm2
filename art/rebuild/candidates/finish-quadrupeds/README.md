# Staged quadruped rebuild

Bighorn revision 5, Tapir revision 4 and Lynx revision 6 GLBs remain staged outside public. Bighorn and Tapir bytes are frozen. Lynx source revision 7 is ready but has not been exported or viewed. No asset in this package is finally accepted or world-integrated.

Root retained the Bighorn/Tapir direction after their hardware views and lifecycle proof. A fresh critic then rejected both against the final art bar: toy-like eyes, smooth materials, poorly integrated extremities, and insufficient anatomical definition. This disagreement remains explicit; root owns the promotion decision. `promotion-catalogue.json` contains the exact normalized frozen pair and file map, and the promotion dry-run passed. Do not apply it as an acceptance claim.

`catalogue-visual-audit.json` and `.md` cover all 29 animals/reptiles. Three reviewers inspected all 145 front, side, rear, gameplay and run PNGs. Every hardware shard reports NVIDIA RTX 5080 Direct3D11, no page errors and no missing assets. KEEP means retain the base for repair and runtime review. The original source/license/geometry audit remains in `source-audit.json` and `.md`.

The procedural horse, fox, Lynx, badger, porcupine, Moose and monitor need structural revision. Legacy cattle and aurochs need seam and identity repairs. Most other imported bases are retainable with the specific issues listed in the visual audit. Stationary run frames establish neither planted travel nor transition quality.

Bighorn uses explicit torso/skull planes, tapered ridged horns and split wedge hooves. Tapir uses continuous authored axial sections with a lower shoulder and smaller rounded ears. `anatomy-revision4.md` records authoritative anatomy references and authored proportion interpretations. `catalogue.json` records pivots and clip metadata. Bighorn maximum ankle error is 0.354 mm; Tapir maximum sampled drawn-sole error is 0.312 mm. Lynx source revision 7 passes all eight whole-skinned-mesh clip audits, with maximum Run penetration 7.7 mm. These are flat-plane source checks.

Natural production lifecycle evidence is in `test-results/quadruped-lifecycle/cairn_bighorn-settled/` and `bracken_tapir-settled/`. Both include real attacks, health loss, movement, turning, kill rewards, death and ordinary respawn. No forced kill or shifted clock was used. Bighorn's automated report passes. Tapir's preserved report fails its old moving-only turn summary; `trace-reanalysis.json` independently verified 3.6127566302618868 radians of meaningful natural heading changes in the same trace, including stationary turns. Its report hash matches. This correction does not prove curved-path travel or supply the final error assertions skipped by the failed run. Both reports retain `visualAccepted:false`.

All 16 lifecycle images and ten matching static views were independently inspected. Death images show mid-collapse actors intersecting their loot crates, not final resting poses. Some screenshot names catch the adjacent animation state; the trace establishes the transition. Continuous foot locking and final-world encounters remain unproved.
