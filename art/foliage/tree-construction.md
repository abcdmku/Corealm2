# Tree construction research

September 7, 2026. Construction brief for the 24 Corealm tree models. These are game-scale interpretations of the reference species, not growth or forestry simulations.

## Why the repeated forks looked wrong

The previous generator placed five to seven initial boughs in the last quarter of a short trunk, then allowed any nearby growth point to sprout repeatedly. Smoothing preserved those junctions. It removed small bends but left the same many-pronged shape throughout the crown.

[Palubicki et al., *Self-organizing tree models for image synthesis*, 2009](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/selforg.sig2009.html) combines local branching rules with competition and apical control. The main stem's advantage over laterals matters to tree form; reducing that advantage with age produces mature crowns. [TreeSketch, Longay et al., 2012](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/TreeSketch.SBM2012.html) separates branching angle, tropism, straightness, bud survival and pruning. A crown envelope alone cannot supply all those rules.

The implementation should preserve a continuing limb through a junction, attach smaller laterals at separate positions, and vary which buds survive. A few large competing stems are plausible. Repeating equal stems at almost every junction is the defect. The [International Society of Arboriculture's young-tree guide](https://www.treesaregood.org/Portals/0/TreesAreGood_Pruning%20Young%20Trees_0621.pdf) distinguishes dominant leaders, scaffold branches and codominant stems, and explains the structural role of relative diameter and spacing. Its pruning recommendations describe managed trees, not a rule that all wild trees have one perfect leader.

Opposite and alternate describe bud/leaf positions. They do not require every adult branch to survive or grow equally. See the [University of Minnesota tree identification cards](https://trees.umn.edu/sites/trees.umn.edu/files/2021-06/UMN_TreeID_CardSet_2021.pdf).

## Construction and acceptance

Use connected wood axes, uneven growth sections, staggered lateral origins and variable branch lengths. Most laterals remain smaller than their parent. Selected mature forms divide into two substantial stems, with comparable diameters and different directions; older oak and magic forms can divide again. Each main stem supports its own smaller boughs. The model seed and species habit choose that architecture before ordinary shoot growth, so every junction does not become an equal fork.

Render the trunk continuously through one daughter at each main division, carrying its full width into a gradual taper. The sibling emerges through a flared collar. Separate growth records still describe the two stems, but the surface must not end in a flat trunk cap beneath two narrower cylinders.

A substantial daughter starts close to its parent's heading before opening into its first growth extension. Limit this initial section by the parent radius, then round the collar into the existing uneven stem sections. This softens the takeoff without replacing the limb with one broad curve. Fine laterals retain their independent departure directions.

Main-stem sections change heading at uneven heights above the clear lower bole. Limbs have a few local elbows with bounded tangents, not one simple arc or high-frequency bends. Thick wood rounds its turns across adjoining growth sections, while fine shoots can bend more tightly. Reserve strong gravity response for willow's thin shoots. Put foliage along leaf-bearing growth, not only in identical stars at every terminal. Preserve existing species textures, magic materials, IDs and grounded placement envelopes. These construction choices are art direction informed by the references above, rather than measured probabilities of a biological growth model.

Inspect every variant fully leafed in the production feature lab, then inspect the wood without leaves. Check multiple views for repeated junctions, radial symmetry, floating foliage and implausible taper. Geometry, source review and screenshots are complementary checks. Run real canvas hover/click and log receipt through production gathering before world integration.

Research scope covered growth architecture, each reference species, and age/habit distinctions. Primary botany and original modeling research supplied the material claims. Further generic searches would not settle the art: the remaining acceptance question is the rendered model. Reference photographs guide construction and are not shipped as game assets.
