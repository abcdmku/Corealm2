# Archived ash creature texture sources

These maps and prompts preserve the provenance of earlier ash creature derivatives. The staging generator and its browser runner were retired when production assets adopted independent rigs. Do not rebuild those old bodies from current production assets.

Current candidates, source pins and acceptance status are recorded in `docs/creature-audit-handoff.md` and the corresponding `assets/art/tripo/imports/creatures/audit-polish-*` catalogs. Reuse the persistent production feature lab for visual acceptance. `tests/ash-creature-redesigns.test.ts` validates the shipped bodies, their rigged action clips and texture bindings.
